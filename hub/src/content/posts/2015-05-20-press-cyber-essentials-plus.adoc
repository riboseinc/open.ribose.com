*Ribose becomes first collaboration platform to complete UK Cyber Essentials Plus*

*XX May 2015 – London / Hong Kong –* Ribose has become the world’s first collaboration platform to complete the UK https://www.cyberstreetwise.com/cyberessentials[Cyber Essentials Plus] assessment, a UK Government-backed and industry-supported scheme that helps to protect businesses against cyber threats.

Launched in June 2014, https://www.cyberstreetwise.com/cyberessentials[Cyber Essentials] was developed by https://web.archive.org/web/20130118023207/https://www.cesg.gov.uk/[CESG] (the information security arm of http://www.gchq.gov.uk[GCHQ]) and the https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-business-innovation-skills[Department of Business&#44; Innovation and Skills] in conjunction with http://www.bsigroup.com[BSI (British Standards Institution)], the http://www.iasme.co.uk/[Information Assurance for Small and Medium Enterprises (IASME) consortium] and the https://www.securityforum.org/[Information Security Forum (ISF)]. Since October 2014, Cyber Essentials certification has been mandatory for UK central government ICT (Information and Communications Technology) suppliers that handle sensitive and personal information.

A two-level program, Cyber Essentials certification is awarded upon completion of a self-assessment questionnaire with independent verification. Cyber Essential Plus certification is optional, and provides a higher level of assurance through independent security controls testing by an external certifying body, focusing on resilience against external cyber threats.

“Ribose is proud to be the world’s first collaboration platform to achieve both Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essential Plus certification,” commented Ronald Tse, founder of Ribose. “After being awarded our Cyber Essentials certification by IASME in August 2014, we were excited to receive Cyber Essential Plus certification accredited by http://www.crest-approved.org/[CREST] following an independent assessment by Cyberis.”

“Cyber Essentials Plus provides independent, third-party verification of our security systems and controls, and also demonstrates our commitment to information security – especially in respect of the UK market,” Tse explained.

According to Geoff Jones, Director of Cyberis, "Cyberis is pleased to award a successful pass certificate to Ribose under the http://www.crest-approved.org/industry-government/cyber-essentials/[CREST Cyber Essentials Plus scheme]. This shows a clear commitment to information security within Ribose, and demonstrates fundamental technical security controls are in place to help defend against Internet-borne threats."
