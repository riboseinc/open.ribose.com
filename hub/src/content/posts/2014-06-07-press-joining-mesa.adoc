*Bringing effective and efficient collaboration to the Media and Entertainment industry*

*Hong Kong, XX March, 2014 –* http://www.ribose.com[Ribose], a start-up redefining the way people collaborate, has joined the http://www.cdsaonline.org/[Media and Entertainment Services Alliance (MESA)], alongside 20^th^ Century Fox, Sony and the Walt Disney Studios.

Ribose’s mission is to help people work together efficiently, effectively and enjoyably. By joining MESA Ribose shows commitment to supporting users in the media and entertainment industry.

Media and entertainment production is inherently collaborative. Improving the way people collaborate saves them time and allows them to devote more energy to putting out the best possible content.

Guy Finley, executive director of the MESA, enthused about the alliance's new member. He said, "We are delighted to welcome Ribose, a pioneering cloud service provider, to MESA. We welcome Ribose’s strong commitment in providing services for the media and entertainment industry. We look forward to working with Ribose to contributing towards our goal of delivering more innovative and empowering solutions to the industry.”

“The Ribose platform is a perfect fit for media and entertainment users," said Ronald Tse, founder of Ribose. “MESA is the perfect forum for Ribose to work closely with the media and entertainment industry to deliver more value through innovative collaboration solutions. We hope to help the media and entertainment industry deliver better content by giving them better tools to collaborate more effectively with one another.”

*About Ribose*

Founded in 2008, Ribose has redefined collaboration with its effective and efficient platform. Whether you are planning social events or running business projects, you will find that Ribose makes collaborating easy and fun. Ribose is free to use: http://www.ribose.com.

*About MESA*

The Media and Entertainment Services Alliance (MESA) is a non-profit organization representing companies that provide services to the media and entertainment industries. Among its members are 20^th^ Century Fox, Adobe, Sony and the Walt Disney Studios. MESA’s goal is to support, promote its members as well as help them collaborate more effectively with one another and with their customers and suppliers.
