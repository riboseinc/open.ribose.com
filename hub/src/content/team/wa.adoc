Koon Wa Kwan is Ribose's Operations Lead where he heads security and service management of the Ribose platform. Graduated from the University of Hong Kong with a bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, he has over two decades software engineering experience especially in workflow and infrastructure automation as well as in governmental solutions.

He is an expert delegate to ISO/TC 154 and ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 40 representing Hong Kong and is most interested in improving cloud service delivery processes using best practices through software and DevOps and DevSecOps methods.

Koon Wa is a member of the itSMF, ACM and IEEE, and is a lead auditor in quality management systems (ISO 9001), IT service management systems (ISO/IEC 20000-1) and information security management systems (ISO/IEC 27001), as well as a Scrum.org PSM and PSD.
