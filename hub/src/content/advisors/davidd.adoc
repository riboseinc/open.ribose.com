Dave focuses on technology and sustainability strategy, and co-authored
"`Citizen Engineer`". Currently Vice President at Applied Minds, Dave was
previously CEO at TouchTable, Chief Sustainability Officer and Senior Vice
President of Cloud Computing at Sun Microsystems. With a BS and MSEE from the
Massachusetts Institute of Technology, he serves on the Boards of the National
Ecological Observatory Network, TouchTable and SkySQL, and is a Senior Fellow
at the Breakthrough Institute.
