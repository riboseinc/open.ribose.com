Prof. Cheung is the Associate Dean of Engineering, Professor of the Department
of Computer Science and also Director of CECID of the University of Hong Kong.
Specializing in databases, data mining and e-commerce, he is widely published
at leading conferences including SIGMOD, VLDB, ICDE and KDD. He serves on the
Board of the Hong Kong Deposit Protection Scheme, the RGC Engineering Panel, as
Chairman of the Certification Board of HKITPC, with previous appointments to
the Hong Kong Central Policy Unit and the PECC.
