Mr. Hamilton serves on the Boards of a number of listed companies including
CITIC Pacific, COSCO International Holdings, Esprit Holdings, Shangri-La Asia
and JPMorgan China Region Fund. He also serves on the Boards of Octopus Cards
Ltd. and DBS Bank (HK) Ltd. A member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants
of Scotland, Hong Kong Institute of Certified Public Accountants and Institute
of Directors, he was a partner in PricewaterhouseCoopers for 16 years and has
more than 30 years of audit and accounting experience.
