A++.++ Rama Krishna, CFA, is Founder and Chief Investment Officer of ARGA
Investment Management, LP, a valuation-based manager of global equity
strategies based in Connecticut and Chennai, India. Prior to the founding of
ARGA, he was President - International at Pzena Investment Management, Chief
Investment Officer, Head of Institutional and International at Citigroup Asset
Management, a member of the Citigroup Management Committee, and held
Directorships and Chief Investment Officer roles at AllianceBernstein in New
York, London and Tokyo. He holds a joint MBA/MA in Asian Studies from
University of Michigan and a BA (Honors) in Economics from St. Stephen's
College, University of Delhi.
