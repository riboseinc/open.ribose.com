== Introduction

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer by websites that
you visit. They are widely used in order to make websites work, or work more
efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

This page describes the cookies we use for our service and why.

== Disabling cookies

You may set your browser to refuse cookies. However, some Services may not
function properly if cookies are disabled.

== More information

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please email {{
site.emails.privacy }}.

== List of cookies and their usage

[cols=2*]
|===
| identifier:
| **_r**

| type:
| Session associated with Ribose Services

| duration:
| 1 week if you checked "Remember Me" option in Login page, otherwise deleted when the browser closes

| description:
| Stores a unique identifier for the session of each computer or device using Ribose. Does not track any personal information, and is required for Services to function correctly.
|===

[cols=2*]
|===
| identifier:
| **client_id**

| type:
| Session associated with Ribose Services

| duration:
| Expired with session

| description:
| Stores a unique identifier for the session of each computer or device using Ribose. Does not track any personal information, and is required for Services to function correctly.
|===

[cols=2*]
|===
| identifier:
| **PREF**

| type:
| Preference cookie associated with Google

| duration:
| 2 years

| description:
| The PREF cookie may store your Google preferences and other information, in
particular your preferred language (e.g., English), how many search results you
wish to have shown per page (e.g., 10 or 20), and whether or not you wish to
have Google's SafeSearch filter turned on.
|===

[cols=2*]
|===
| identifier:
| **\_ga / _gid / _gat / AMP_TOKEN / _gac_<property-id>**

| type:
| Preference cookie associated with Google Analytics

| duration:
| Maximum 2 years

| description:
| These cookies record particular ID used to come up with data about website usage by the visitors.
|===

[cols=2*]
|===
| identifier:
| **invitation_code**

| type:
| Token cookie associated with Ribose

| duration:
| 5 minutes

| description:
| This cookie records the code of invitation.
|===

[cols=2*]
|===
| identifier:

| type:
| Token cookie associated with Ribose

| duration:
| 5 minutes

| description:

[cols=2*]
|===
| identifier:
| **rpt**

| type:

| duration:
| 5 minutes

| description:
| This cookie records the token for password reset.
|===

[cols=2*]
|===
| identifier:

| type:
