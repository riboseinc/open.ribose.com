Cloud collaboration platform provider Ribose has been named a winner of the
http://www.redherring.com/events/red-herring-asia/2015-top100-winners/[Red Herring 2015 Top 100 Asia] Award.

Ribose has been named a winner of the Red Herring 2015 Top 100 Asia Award and
is the first secure cloud collaboration company to be included in global media
conglomerate Red Herring's annual lineup of rising stars in the technology
world.

http://www.redherring.com/events/red-herring-asia/[Red Herring's Top 100 Asia] is a prestigious recognition
for the region's most promising private technology ventures. The list has
become a mark of distinction for up-and-coming companies and entrepreneurs,
and Ribose joins prominent previous Top 100 winners including Google,
Facebook, Kakao, Twitter, Rakuten, Salesforce.com, Xiaomi and YouTube.

Choosing from a pool of hundreds of companies across Asia each year, the Red
Herring editorial team selects the most innovative candidates for further
assessment. Nominees are evaluated on both quantitative and qualitative
criteria including financial performance, technological innovation, quality of
management, execution of strategy and integration into their respective
industries. Ribose was named a winner after presenting its secure cloud
collaboration platform to judges at the Red Herring Asia Forum held in Manila
on 14-16 September 2015.
