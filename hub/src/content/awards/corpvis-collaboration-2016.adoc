Technology Innovator Awards for allowing users in security-aware industries to
work together effectively, while protecting their highly-valuable collaborative
data through pioneering cloud security and privacy, all delivered through an
intuitive user experience.

Other winners include leading technology providers such as 3M, BT, CA
Technologies, KPMG, Tripwire, and Xerox.
