Ribose was named winner of two Gold Stevie® Awards in the
https://web.archive.org/web/20160613113328/http://stevieawards.com/iba/ribose-inc-hong-kong-new-product["Best New Product or Service of the Year -- Software Collaboration/Social Networking Solution"]
and
https://web.archive.org/web/20160613113328/http://stevieawards.com/iba/ribose-inc-hong-kong["Most Innovative Company of the Year in Asia, Australia and New Zealand"]
categories in The 12th Annual International Business Awards.

Ribose was named winner of two Gold Stevie® Awards in the
"https://web.archive.org/web/20160613113328/http://stevieawards.com/iba/ribose-inc-hong-kong-new-product[Best New Product or Service of the Year -- Software Collaboration/Social Networking Solution]" and
"https://web.archive.org/web/20160613113328/http://stevieawards.com/iba/ribose-inc-hong-kong[Most Innovative Company of the Year in Asia, Australia and New Zealand]"
categories in http://stevieawards.com/iba/[The 12th Annual International Business Awards].

http://stevieawards.com/iba/[The International Business Awards] are the world's premier business awards
program. All individuals and organizations worldwide -- public and private,
for-profit and non-profit, large and small -- are eligible to submit
nominations. The 2015 IBAs received entries from more than 60 nations and
territories. More than 3,700 nominations from organizations of all sizes and
in virtually every industry were submitted this year for consideration in a
wide range of categories.

IBA Judges commended the Ribose platform for democratizing secure
collaboration, by providing a "technically robust offering" with "collaboration
tools in a secured setting", allowing unprecedented flexibility to use the
"open platform" in both "professional working and fun" settings through a "very
easy to use and attractive interface design" -- as winning factors in naming
Ribose's platform the
https://web.archive.org/web/20160613113328/http://stevieawards.com/iba/ribose-inc-hong-kong-new-product[Best New Product or Service of the Year -- Software Collaboration/Social Networking Solution]
category. Ribose's security focus was best summarized by a judge who noted:
"Most existing cloud collaboration software simply does not offer the necessary
security levels to protect mission-critical, commercially sensitive projects."

In naming Ribose the
https://web.archive.org/web/20160613113328/http://stevieawards.com/iba/ribose-inc-hong-kong[Most Innovative Company of the Year in Asia, Australia and New Zealand],
IBA Judges lauded the company's "great commitment and great team, deploying the
technology to the benefit of both the company and the community," "professional
management" and "excellent performance", as well as its "impressive
accomplishment in achieving multiple certifications", providing users with "all
the benefits of an enterprise-class collaboration platform, with none of the
associated infrastructure and costs."
